# Stage 3 diagnostics: score model and simulation

Generated by `cfbsim stage3`. FBS vs FBS games only. Data provided by CollegeFootballData.com.

Settings chosen on 3,616 games from 2016-20: `{'halflife': 8.0, 'season_weight': 0.5}`, ridge alpha 300.0, bandwidth 3.0.
Diagnosed on 1,546 games from [2021, 2022], which were not used for selection. The 2023-25 holdout was not touched.

Listed betting lines are benchmarks only and never model inputs. CFBD doesn't say when in the week they were captured, so they are not described as closing lines.

## Overtime and repeatability

Overtime rate: actual 4.5%, simulated 4.2%, separate draws 2.8%.

Two independent 10,000-game runs differ by 0.46% in win probability on average (largest 1.94%). That is only simulation noise, not forecast accuracy.

Correlation between the two teams after allowing for expected points: 0.022 in training, 0.004 on the diagnostic seasons.
